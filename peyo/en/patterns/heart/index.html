Make it your own in Peyo

  1. Start a blank design with 12 columns and 10 rows. Redraw the labeled cells, using three colors from your palette.
  2. Check the total and color counts against the table. Changing a color does not change the number of beads.
  3. Export your design as an image or a project card. Drawing, counting and image export are free; no account is required.

Optional: Peyo Pro lets you draw over a reference image and replace a color throughout a pattern. You can also redraw this chart by hand with the free drawing tools. Check saving options and prices in the app.

About this chart

This is a design chart, not step-by-step bead-stitching instructions. Adjacent columns are shifted by half a bead, matching Peyo’s peyote canvas. Use your preferred flat-peyote stitching instructions for thread paths and finishing.
