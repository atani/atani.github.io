Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Overview

Match Notebook is a racket-sports notebook that stores your data on your device. Opponents, matches, strategies, and related notes remain on the device you use.

Data Collection

Match Notebook does not collect, transmit, or store personal data on developer-operated servers. It requires no account, contains no advertising, and includes no third-party analytics or tracking SDK. Core features work offline.

Data Stored on Your Device

  • Opponent profiles, notes, and weaknesses
  • Match dates, locations, scores, and notes
  • Strategy cards
  • App preferences such as the default sport

This data is stored locally using iOS file protection.

App Store and Purchases

Apple processes in-app purchases through the App Store. Match Notebook does not handle or store payment information. StoreKit communicates with Apple when loading product information or processing a purchase. Apple may provide the developer with aggregated App Store metrics such as downloads, purchases, and usage.

Sharing and Deletion

Match Notebook does not share your notebook data with third parties and has no server-side component. You can delete stored app data by uninstalling the app from your device.

Future Features

If optional iCloud sync is added in a future update, it will use Apple CloudKit and your personal iCloud account. Data will not pass through developer-operated servers.
